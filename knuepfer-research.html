Research

My research lies at the interface of the calculus of variations and partial differential equations. I study variational problems arising in materials science and mathematical physics, with a focus on the asymptotic and scaling behaviour of energy minimisers.

Calculus of Variations

Existence, regularity, and asymptotic analysis of energy minimisers; Γ-convergence and sharp interface limits.

Free Boundary Problems

Well-posedness and lubrication approximations for thin-film equations; Navier-slip boundary conditions; wetting and dewetting.

Micromagnetics

Domain structures and Néel walls in thin ferromagnetic films; ferromagnetic crystals in applied fields; perpendicular anisotropy.

Isoperimetric Problems

Isoperimetric problems with competing nonlocal terms; phase separation in charged droplets; Ohta–Kawasaki type energies.

Phase Transformations

Nucleation barriers for cubic-to-tetragonal phase transformations in shape memory alloys; elastic inclusions.

Lattice and Particle Systems

Minimal energy for lattices with charge distributions; crystallisation for alternating particle chains; optimal lattice configurations.
