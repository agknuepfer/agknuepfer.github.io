Section

The Applied Analysis section at the Institute for Mathematics, Heidelberg University brings together research groups working on partial differential equations, calculus of variations, geometric analysis, and mathematical modelling in the natural sciences. We share interests in the rigorous analysis of complex systems, pattern formation, free boundary problems, and the derivation of effective models across scales.

Joint activities include the Applied Analysis Oberseminar and a PhD Discussion Group open to Master's and Bachelor's students.

Working Groups

Calculus of Variations and PDEs

Prof. Dr. Hans Knüpfer

Variational problems in materials science and fluid dynamics; pattern formation, nonlocal interactions, free boundary problems.
