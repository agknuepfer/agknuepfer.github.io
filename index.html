About

I am a Professor of Mathematics in the Applied Analysis group at the Institut für Mathematik, Heidelberg University.

My research lies at the interface of the calculus of variations and partial differential equations, with applications to materials science, micromagnetics, and fluid dynamics. I am particularly interested in asymptotic and scaling analysis, free boundary problems, and isoperimetric problems with nonlocal competing terms.

Together with AG Marciniak-Czochra, I organise a PhD Discussion Group open to Bachelor's and Master's students at the institute.

Research Interests

  • Calculus of variations and variational methods
  • Partial differential equations — well-posedness, regularity, asymptotics
  • Free boundary problems and thin-film equations
  • Micromagnetics: domain structures, Néel walls, ferromagnetic films
  • Isoperimetric problems with nonlocal competing terms
  • Phase transformations in shape memory alloys
  • Γ-convergence and sharp interface limits
  • Charged particle systems and lattice energy problems
