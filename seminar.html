About the Seminar

The Applied Analysis Seminar is a weekly research seminar of the Applied Analysis section at the Institute for Mathematics, Heidelberg University. It covers topics from partial differential equations, calculus of variations, geometric analysis, and mathematical modelling.

Time and place — Thursdays, 14:15–15:15, Mathematikon (INF 205), Room 5/104.
