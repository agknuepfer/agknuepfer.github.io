About the Oberseminar

The Applied Analysis Oberseminar is a weekly research seminar of the Applied Analysis section at the Institute for Mathematics, Heidelberg University. It covers topics from partial differential equations, calculus of variations, geometric analysis, and mathematical modelling.

Time and place — Thursdays, 14:15–15:15, Mathematikon (INF 205), Room 5/104.
Announcements are distributed via the mailing list. To be added, write to .
