How to Find Us

The Applied Analysis section is located in the Mathematikon, Heidelberg's mathematics and computer science building on the Neuenheimer Feld science campus. The campus is easily reached by tram (line 21/24, stop Bunsengymnasium or Jahnstraße) or bus from Heidelberg city centre.
