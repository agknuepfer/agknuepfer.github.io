Teaching

The Applied Analysis section offers courses in analysis, partial differential equations, calculus of variations, and mathematical modelling at all levels of the mathematics curriculum. The core courses for students interested in applied analysis are Partial Differential Equations, Functional Analysis, and Ordinary Differential Equations.

The section's research seminar is the Applied Analysis Oberseminar — a weekly research seminar covering topics from PDEs, calculus of variations, geometric analysis, and mathematical modelling.
