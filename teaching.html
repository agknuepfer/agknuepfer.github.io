Applied Analysis
Section · Institute for Mathematics · Heidelberg University
Overview Research Teaching Oberseminar

The Applied Analysis section offers courses in analysis and PDEs at all levels of the mathematics curriculum. The Bachelor lectures on Ordinary Differential Equations, Functional Analysis, and Partial Differential Equations form the foundation for the section's graduate courses and are strongly recommended before attending Master-level offerings.

At the graduate level the section regularly offers, in most years, courses on PDE in Data Science, Calculus of Variations, and PDE for Pattern Formation. In addition to formal lecture courses, the section runs research seminars and an informal PhD discussion group open to students at all levels.
